## Offline reading tip

If some of these sites are blocked where you work, download the PDFs at home (`https://arxiv.org/pdf/<id>`) into a local `papers/` folder. AI models also analyze attached PDFs far more reliably than fetched links, which matters for the [analysis prompt](../../prompts/patterns/research-to-project-analysis.md).
